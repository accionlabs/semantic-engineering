## Track Record

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Legacy code modernized | More than 15 million lines, across ASP.NET, COBOL, Delphi, ASP.NET Web Forms, VB.NET and proprietary systems |
| Programs delivered | More than ten, in large enterprises and regulated industries |
| Industries | Insurance and finance, healthcare and life sciences, inventory and logistics, education technology, fuel and billing |

## Programs

| Program | Result |
|---|---|
| Java 8 to Java 21, inventory and warehouse | 2.1 million lines in about three and a half months, with outdated APIs found and replaced automatically |
| Delphi to .NET 8, education technology | About 3 million lines in about eight months; about 60% less effort than manual modernization |
| ASP.NET Web Forms to .NET Core 8, financial services | About 50% less effort than manual; transactions 20% faster; unit test coverage above 80% |
| COBOL on AS/400 to .NET 8, insurance | 600,000 lines; mainframe and scarce-skill dependency removed; migration run in parallel streams |
| Struts to Spring MVC, healthcare software | About 3.2 million lines in about five months |

The programs are described in [Case Archetypes: Legacy Modernization](../modernization/case-archetypes.md).

## Indicative Outcomes

On a standalone codebase of one million lines, up to four times faster than manual modernization, and up to 70% less migration time. Actual outcomes depend on the scope, the target stack and the modules selected.

> **To confirm with the ASIMOV lead:** the total lines modernized. This site says more than 15 million; the sales decks say more than 6 million.
