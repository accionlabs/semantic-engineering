ASIMOV is used within an Accion Labs modernization engagement. A client can start at any of five modes and stop at any of them, or move from one to the next as confidence grows. The earlier modes have a fixed scope and a fixed price; later modes move to outcome-based pricing.

## Ways to Start

| Offer | What the client gets | Duration | Pricing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Modernization Assessment** | Reconstructed knowledge of the system, a target architecture, a phased plan, and a cost and risk estimate | | Fixed price |

> **To confirm with leadership and the ASIMOV lead:** the assessment's duration, and "from" prices for the assessment and for Documentation Only, to publish on this page.

## The Five Modes

| Mode | What it delivers | Typical duration | Pricing model |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Documentation Only** | Documentation of the legacy system from its code: business requirements, test scenarios, end-to-end test scripts and a code chat. No migration. | Two to six weeks | Fixed price, from the size of the legacy system |
| **Discovery and Documentation** | The above, plus an application inventory, dependency maps, business rules, process flows and review packs | Four to ten weeks | Fixed price, or time and materials, from the size of the system and how much expert review is needed |
| **Migration Readiness** | Migration scope by module, what stays and what changes, target mapping, sequencing, a risk and complexity view, and a requirements traceability matrix | Six to twelve weeks | Fixed price, from the modules in scope |
| **Full Modernization** | The full pipeline: discovery, specification, migration, the four quality gates and deployment, in waves | Quarters to years, by the size of the estate | Time and materials with milestones by module, moving to outcome-based as the program settles |
| **Maintain, Operate and Convergence** | Ongoing knowledge of the new system, change support, and analysis of overlap across products | Continuous | Outcome-based, tied to an agreed service level |

The activities in each mode are described in [Engagement Modes of Legacy Modernization](../modernization/engagement-modes/_index.md).

## What Affects the Price

- The size of the legacy system, in lines of code and number of applications.
- The source and target stacks.
- How many modules are in scope.
- How much review the client's experts need.
- The client's path to production.
