## Delivery on a Multi-Product Engagement

Three products, delivered by the client's existing team over a 14-week pilot. Measured at the start and at the end of the pilot.

| Measure | Before | End of pilot | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deployments a month | 19 | 36 | 90% more |
| Lead time for changes | 2.0 days | 1.62 days | 19% shorter |

Source: Breeze.AI Executive Leadership Report, Q3 2026 (figures as at 20 October 2026). The full table, including figures based on the client's own estimates, is in [Delivery Outcomes on a Multi-Product Engagement](../sdlc/case-archetypes.md#delivery-outcomes-on-a-multi-product-engagement).
