Breeze.AI is licensed as part of an Accion Labs engagement. Each phase has its own pricing model, and the price is set from the size of the client's application and the number of teams and products.

## Ways to Start

| Offer | What the client gets | Duration | Pricing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deep-dive workshop** | An adoption plan, a first draft of the graph's structure, and a twelve-week roadmap, from the client's own context | Two days | Fixed price |
| **System Model Pilot** | The client's application in a four-layer graph, and live impact analysis on a real item from the client's backlog | Two to three weeks | Fixed price |

> **To confirm with leadership:** "from" prices for the workshop and the System Model Pilot, to publish on this page.

## Pricing by Phase

| Phase | What happens | Pricing model | What the licence covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Advise** | Workshop and pilot on one application | Fixed price | Platform access for the workshop and pilot |
| **Launch** | The first workstream adopts the method; the checks are switched on | Time and materials, with deliverables tied to milestones; mostly part-time roles | Light integration and links to the ticket system, then the full platform for the first workstream |
| **Scale** | More workstreams and products | Time and materials, moving to outcome-based as the work settles | The full platform across the portfolio |
| **Optimize** | The platform runs; Accion Labs keeps it current | Outcome-based, tied to an agreed service level | The full platform across the portfolio |

See the [SDLC Engagement Model](../sdlc/engagement-model.md) for the activities, people and deliverables in each phase.

## What Affects the Price

- The size of the application, in lines of code and number of repositories.
- The number of products and teams.
- The deployment option: hosted by Accion Labs, or in the client's environment.
- How much of the ongoing work the client's own team takes on.

Write to [engage@accionlabs.com](mailto:engage@accionlabs.com) with the application's size, the number of teams and products, and where the platform would need to run. The reply confirms the scope and proposes a one-hour scoping call before a price is shared. See [Contact](../practitioner/contact.md).
